2. Intended use and responsibilities

RetailX is an experimental local tool for recording store inventory, stock movements, alert conditions, queue-region observations, and reports. Use it only on a computer and with data and video you are authorized to process. Keep the server on loopback, protect the local data folder, review imported CSV data, and keep backups before changing or removing stored records. No authentication, multi-user permissions, or shared-store isolation is implemented.

3. Estimates and accuracy

Object-detection results depend on camera placement, image quality, lighting, occlusion, encoding, model behavior, and sampling interval. A sampled frame is one observation; the same person may be counted repeatedly. Queue counts are detections with a bounding-box center inside the configured region. RetailX does not identify people, infer demographics or dwell time, calculate reliable waiting time, or guarantee stock levels. Verify estimates before using them for staffing, safety, purchasing, or customer decisions.

4. Availability and technical limits

The application is provided as a local prototype and may contain defects, stop unexpectedly, or require compatible packages, model weights, codecs, storage, and compute resources. The retention control is currently a preference and does not delete data automatically. Backups and recovery are the operator’s responsibility.
